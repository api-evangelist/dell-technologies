AI demand is straining DRAM and NAND supply chains, making efficiency-first infrastructure architectures better hedged against disruption. Dell frames supply chain constraints as a cyber resilience question, arguing that organizations relying on commodity memory at scale face compounding risk from both shortages and security vulnerabilities.
